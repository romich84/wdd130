Find Your Next Adventure

Whether you are looking for a relaxing introduction to rafting or an exciting challenge on the river, Rafting Argentina offers experiences for different interests and adventure levels. Explore our trips and choose your next unforgettable experience!

River Explorer

Discover the excitement of white water rafting while enjoying beautiful natural landscapes. This introductory experience is ideal for visitors who want to discover rafting and enjoy an outdoor adventure with a group.

  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Difficulty: Beginner
  • Experience: Introduction to rafting

Rapid Challenge

Looking for more excitement? Experience the thrill of navigating challenging rapids and working together as a team. Enjoy an energetic adventure surrounded by impressive river scenery.

  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Difficulty: Intermediate
  • Experience: Action and teamwork

Full-Day Adventure

Make the most of your day exploring the river and its surroundings. This longer experience combines rafting, beautiful scenery, and time outdoors for visitors looking for a memorable adventure.

  • Duration: 6 hours
  • Difficulty: Advanced
  • Experience: Full-day river adventure
